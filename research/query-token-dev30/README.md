An independent unpromoted candidate, not the online service or the frozen dev29 repair study. Eighteen of the parent's twenty modules are identical. Only the version and reranker query-token selection change, plus one pure helper. Short and unsupported inputs keep the parent's head/tail view; bounded complete middle technical names may replace a limited part of a long query's existing192tokens. Query and evidence text are not rewritten, generated, executed or logged by the service.

Thirteen owned checks exercise the behavior and the original16documents/threewindows/512tokens inference limits. Twelve self-authored relevance probes use the same six pinned MiniLM assets and dependencies for both arms on one free runner. They contain no benchmark questions, histories, target tests, answers or credentials. The prospective relevance gate requires at least two net wins on six changed probes, no loss of any previously correct case, and identical scores on all six unchanged probes. Completion without that benefit is reported separately. This cannot prove real coding repair benefit or Full readiness.
